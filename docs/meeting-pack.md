## The call script (5 PM)

1. **Requirements (first, no money talk):**
   - What's the project / what does it need to do?
   - Who's it for — main business site or a product?
   - What do you have already (content, branding, domain)?
   - When do you need it live? (deadline = your urgency lever)

2. **Budget ask (then):**
   "What kind of budget did you have in mind for this?"

3. **Price rule:**
   - No budget stated → give range anchored high: "$2k-4k depending on scope" → let them react
   - Budget stated → quote **budget + $100** (their number, slightly up — easy yes)
   - Their budget $1,000-2,000 → **"Done — I can do that."** Close fast, no bargaining.
   - Below $1,000 → bargain UP: "For that I can cover the landing page + core pages — the full build typically runs a bit more. What's most important to you?" → anchor the upsell.

4. **Close:**
   "I'll send an invoice via PayPal tonight — 50% to start, 50% on delivery. I can start tomorrow morning. Sound good?"

## After the call
- Invoice via PayPal immediately (they said yes = invoice in the same hour)
- Confirm next step in chat: "Great call — invoice sent, starting tomorrow. Here's what I'll have for you by [date]."
